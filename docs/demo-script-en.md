# Demo video script, maximum duration: 5 minutes

## 0:00-0:35 - The problem

"We buy food, forget what we already have, let ingredients expire, and sometimes chase a discount without considering the travel cost. nutrIAhorro is an everyday agent that connects nutrition, pantry memory, and savings so those scattered facts become simple decisions in one experience."

Show the Today screen with priorities, macro progress, and estimated savings. Reset the demo before recording.

## 0:35-1:15 - Personal goals

Open Goals and show the goal, weight, activity, exercise, available cooking time, and preferences.

"The plan starts with the person's real context. Their goals become editable calorie and macronutrient references for general wellness, without replacing professional advice."

## 1:15-2:00 - Pantry memory

Upload the fictional sample receipt, review the candidates, correct one item, confirm, and open Pantry.

"A receipt becomes persistent pantry memory only after human review. nutrIAhorro tracks separate batches, flags low stock and items to use soon, and prioritizes the oldest safe ingredient first."

Show chicken, tomato, and avocado as priorities. State that the example uses fictional data.

## 2:00-2:50 - Meals that fit real life

Open Recipes, filter by preparation time, and enter a recipe.

"nutrIAhorro combines the profile, available time, and actual ingredient quantities. Every recipe shows calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat. When I confirm that I cooked it, one action records all four values and deducts the exact pantry quantities."

Confirm a meal, show the updated Today progress, and return to Pantry to verify the deduction.

## 2:50-3:40 - Savings with context

Open Shopping and switch between walking, bicycle, car, and motorcycle.

"The lowest shelf price is not always the best purchase. The agent includes round-trip distance and transport cost. In this demo, it recommends the option with the lowest effective total among nearby stores. All displayed prices are fictional demo data, not live promotions."

## 3:40-4:40 - The Strands agent

Open the assistant and ask: "What should I use first, what can I cook quickly, and where should I shop?"

"The decision layer is built with Strands Agents SDK. Its six tools read the profile, pantry, and daily progress; suggest feasible meals; compare nearby shopping; and register state-changing actions only after confirmation. AgentCore is optional under the rules and is not part of this submission."

Briefly show the architecture diagram and the six tool names in the public repository.

## 4:40-5:00 - Impact

"nutrIAhorro helps busy people and households eat with more awareness, waste less food, and protect their budget. It does not replace health professionals; it turns repetitive food decisions into practical everyday actions."
